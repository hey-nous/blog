OpenAI's models are coming to Google Cloud

GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna are rolling out in private preview on Google Cloud's model garden, billed through Google Cloud Marketplace. What was announced, what is still coming, and why it matters.

22 Oct 2026  •  4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Google Cloud announced on October 21, 2026 that OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna are rolling out in private preview in its model garden.
  • Google Cloud says it is now the only hyperscale provider offering Gemini, Claude, and GPT side by side, alongside more than 200 other models.
  • Billing runs through Google Cloud Marketplace and counts toward existing spending commitments; OpenAI says general availability follows in the coming weeks, with Microsoft remaining its primary cloud partner.
  • For buyers this is the third cloud route to GPT-6 after Microsoft and AWS — the differentiator is getting Gemini on the same platform.

What happened

Google Cloud announced on October 21, 2026 that OpenAI's models are coming to its model garden. Three models are rolling out now in private preview to select enterprise customers: GPT-6 Astra for demanding coding, computer use, and research work; GPT-6.1 Sol, described as near-Astra intelligence at lower cost for agentic coding and automation; and GPT-6 Luna, a fast and cost-efficient model for everyday work at scale.

The announcement was authored by Michael Gerstenhaber, Google Cloud's vice president of product for Gemini. Its core argument: enterprise AI is about matching each workload to the right model, not using one model for everything.

One bill, three model families

The enterprise pitch is procurement, not capability. The models are billed directly through Google Cloud Marketplace, and Google says consumption counts toward customers' existing spending commitments and Flexible Savings Plans. Teams can call the models through Google Cloud's APIs and connect local Codex CLI and desktop workflows to the platform for inference.

Google Cloud's headline claim: it is now the only hyperscale cloud provider with Gemini, Claude, and GPT on one platform, alongside more than 200 first-party, third-party, and open models. Coming soon, according to the announcement: OpenAI models inside Gemini for business and Google Antigravity, its coding tool.

What it means for you

Context matters here. This is the third of the three largest clouds to sell GPT-6, not the first: Microsoft's Foundry listed GPT-6 Astra as generally available on September 3, 2026, and Amazon Bedrock followed on September 8. OpenAI's own post adds two facts buyers need: general availability on Google Cloud is planned for the coming weeks, and Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner.

So the news is not that GPT-6 exists somewhere new. It is what Google Cloud customers specifically gain: no new vendor contract, no separate bill, and the one thing neither Microsoft nor AWS offers — Gemini sitting on the same shelf. If your team already spends on Google Cloud, requesting preview access is now a procurement decision, not an architecture one.

What is still unclear: Google has not published its own prices for these models, so do not assume they match OpenAI's API list prices until your account team confirms. Zero Data Retention and Confidential Compute options are also listed as coming soon, not available today.
