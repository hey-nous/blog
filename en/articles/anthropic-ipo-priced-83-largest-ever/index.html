Anthropic's IPO priced at $83, the largest ever

Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83 on November 18, raising $81.3 billion in the biggest IPO on record. The stock opened at $111.50 the next day and closed up 17%.

20 Nov 2026  •  4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic priced its IPO at $83.00 per share on November 18, 2026, above the $70 to $78 range it set when the roadshow launched on November 10.
  • The offering covers 980 million Class A shares, worth about $81.3 billion, which Reuters calls the largest IPO on record, passing SpaceX's $75 billion base offering.
  • A concurrent $26 billion private placement to seven strategic partners, including Google, Microsoft, and Nvidia, brings the total capital raised to about $107.3 billion.
  • On its first day of trading, November 19, the stock opened at $111.50, touched $115.10, and closed at $97.17, up 17% from the IPO price.

What happened

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83.00 per share on November 18, 2026, above the $70 to $78 range it set when the roadshow launched eight days earlier. The offering covers 980 million shares of Class A common stock: 956,351,354 sold by Anthropic itself and 23,648,646 sold by existing stockholders, from whose shares the company receives no proceeds.

The price sets the base offering at about $81.3 billion. Reuters reports that makes it the largest IPO on record, passing the $75 billion base offering SpaceX raised in June. The registration statement was declared effective by the US Securities and Exchange Commission the same day, and the offering is expected to close on November 20, subject to customary conditions.

The $26 billion sitting next to the IPO

The headline number is not the whole raise. Alongside the public offering, seven strategic partners agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting Class N shares at the same $83 price: Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, and TSMC. Together, the public offering and the placement total about $107.3 billion.

That placement is the part that matters most for Anthropic's business. The seven buyers are its chip suppliers, its cloud hosts, and its largest customers, and the money is tied to the same infrastructure Anthropic needs to train and serve Claude. The underwriters, led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, also hold a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million additional shares, worth about $12.2 billion at the IPO price.

On valuation, the figures depend on which share count you use. Reuters puts Anthropic at about $1.1 trillion on shares outstanding after the offering, and about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis that counts stock options and restricted stock. Anthropic's own announcement gives no valuation figure.

Summary

Anthropic priced its IPO above range at $83, selling 980 million shares for about $81.3 billion, the largest IPO ever. A separate $26 billion private placement to seven chip and cloud partners brings the total to about $107.3 billion.

What it means for you

The first trading day answered the question the pricing left open. Shares began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT on November 19, after co-founders Dario and Daniela Amodei rang the opening bell. The first trade came at about 11:50 a.m. Eastern, at $111.50, about 34% above the IPO price, and briefly put Anthropic's market value near $2 trillion, according to Reuters. The stock touched $115.10, slid as low as $92.72 in the afternoon, and closed at $97.17, up 17% from the offering price.

For anyone who builds with Claude, the listing changes little today. Anthropic's filing puts run-rate revenue at about $81 billion as of October 31, and the company reported an operating loss of about $1.3 billion for the first nine months of 2026, so the capital is going into a business that is growing fast and still spending heavily to do it. API prices and plan limits are unchanged by the listing, and the public market now gets a quarterly look at whether the enterprise business can support the valuation.

Warning

The $83 price is what investors who received an allocation paid. It is not the price most people pay: the first public trade was $111.50, and the stock closed its first day at $97.17. The valuation figures also differ by method, about $1.1 trillion on shares outstanding versus about $1.5 trillion fully diluted, so check which count a source uses before comparing.

What is still unclear: whether the $26 billion placement closes on schedule alongside the offering, and how the market values Anthropic once the first-day excitement settles. The offering closes on November 20, and the first quarterly report as a public company will be the first hard test of the $1.5 trillion figure.
