ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini vs DeepSeek: which one is worth paying for?

Four AI assistants, four pricing philosophies. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and DeepSeek compared on price, free tiers, and what each one is actually best at. Prices checked October 2026.

22 Oct 2026  •  6 min read

Key takeaways

  • The three flagship subscriptions all cost about $20 a month: ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, and Google AI Pro. DeepSeek's chat is completely free with no paid tier at all.
  • Price alone does not settle this. What matters is how generous the free tier is, where the usage limits bite, and which apps each assistant connects to.
  • Rough guide: ChatGPT Plus as the all-rounder, Claude Pro for writing and coding with Claude Code, Google AI Pro if you live in Gmail and Docs, DeepSeek if you want strong AI for free.
  • Prices below are US list prices before tax, checked on October 22, 2026. They change often, so confirm on the official pricing page before you pay.

The short answer

Here is the verdict up front: the three flagship subscriptions all cost about $20 a month, and DeepSeek costs nothing. So the real question is never about price. It is about what that $20 gets you, and whether you need to pay at all.

What $20 actually buys

ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo). OpenAI sells breadth: GPT-6 reasoning models, expanded deep research, Codex for agentic coding, custom GPTs, image creation, voice mode, and ChatGPT Work across desktop, web, and mobile. Everyday text chat is now unlimited on every plan, so Plus is really about the frontier models plus higher limits on everything around them. A cheaper Go tier exists at $8/mo, and Pro tiers start at $100/mo for heavy users.

Claude Pro ($20/mo, or $17/mo billed annually). Anthropic sells depth: careful writing, long-document handling, and Claude Code, the agentic coding tool that now ships inside the Pro subscription. Expect roughly 40 to 45 messages per five-hour window. Claude is the only one of the three with an annual discount for individuals. Max tiers from $100/mo multiply the limits for heavy users.

Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo). Google sells the bundle: Gemini 4 Argon and 3.1 Pro models, plus 5 TB of storage across Gmail, Drive, and Photos, YouTube Premium Lite, 1,000 Flow video credits, and sharing with up to five family members. If your digital life already runs on Google, this is the easiest $20 to justify. A cheaper AI Plus tier exists at $4.99/mo, and Ultra tiers start at $99.99/mo.

DeepSeek ($0). DeepSeek has no paid consumer plan at all. The web and mobile chat is free with full access to its V4 models, including the reasoning model with visible thinking steps. The trade-offs: it is text-only with no image or voice input, weaker at creative writing, and hosted usage stores your data on Chinese servers. Its API is also famously cheap, which matters more if you build with AI than if you just chat with it.

When you should not pay

Most comparisons skip this part. The free tiers in 2026 are genuinely good: ChatGPT's free plan includes unlimited basic text chats, Claude's free tier covers casual daily use, and DeepSeek hands you a frontier-class reasoning model for nothing. If you use AI a few times a week for everyday questions, the free tier is probably enough. Pay when you hit a limit that actually blocks your work: capped messages, locked models, or missing features like deep research.

How to choose

  • Writer or student working with long documents: Claude Pro. Long-context handling and a careful writing style are its edge, and the annual plan brings it down to $17/mo.
  • Coder: Claude Pro for Claude Code, or ChatGPT Plus for Codex. Run both free tiers against a real task before deciding.
  • Living in Gmail, Docs, and Drive: Google AI Pro. The 5 TB of storage and family sharing alone can justify the price.
  • Tight budget: DeepSeek. Free, strong at math and coding, with visible reasoning steps. Keep sensitive data away from the hosted chat.
  • Want one subscription that does a bit of everything: ChatGPT Plus. The broadest feature set, from image generation to custom GPTs.

Before you pay

Three things worth knowing. First, these are US list prices before tax, and they change often. Confirm the number on the official pricing page before checkout. Second, only Claude currently offers an annual discount to individuals. Third, none of these subscriptions include API usage, which is billed separately if you build apps on top of the models.
