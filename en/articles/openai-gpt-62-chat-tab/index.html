GPT-6.2 arrives in ChatGPT's chat tab for paid users

OpenAI is rolling out a chat-tuned GPT-6.2 Sol to the Chat tab for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users. It reads photos better and follows tricky instructions more reliably, but free users wait, and the numbers are OpenAI's own.

25 Nov 2026  •  4 min read

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI started rolling out a chat-tuned GPT-6.2 Sol to ChatGPT's Chat tab on November 23, 2026, for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users; Enterprise access depends on workspace admin settings.
  • In OpenAI's own tests, image understanding rose to 71.7% from 47.4% for GPT-6, and following hard instructions rose to 78.1% from 62.5%.
  • The model can proactively offer follow-ups, such as alerting you when a price drops after you ask whether something is on sale.
  • GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol from November 17 stay in Work and Codex; this release changes only regular Chat. Free and Go users stay on GPT-6 Luna, with no date given.
  • OpenAI will retire GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra on December 23, 2026.

What happened

OpenAI started rolling out a version of GPT-6.2 Sol, tuned for everyday conversation, to ChatGPT's Chat tab on November 23, 2026, for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users worldwide.

Until now, the regular Chat tab still ran on GPT-6. OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on November 17, but only inside ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API, so the chat most people actually open every day was still a generation behind. This update closes that gap, at least for paying users. The rollout is gradual, so it may take a few days to reach your account, and on Enterprise workspaces an admin decides whether it is switched on.

Summary

GPT-6.2 Sol is now rolling out to the ChatGPT Chat tab for paid plans, six days after Astra and Sol arrived in Work and Codex. It is a chat-tuned build, not a new model, and Work and Codex do not change.

What gets better

OpenAI's pitch is everyday complexity: requests with detailed instructions, messy trade-offs, and follow-up questions. Its example is a hotel search for a weekend trip, where ChatGPT weighs price against location and recommends the option that fits your priorities. If you ask whether a product is on sale, it can now offer to tell you when the price drops.

The numbers OpenAI reports from its own Chat tab tests show the biggest jump in reading images. Image understanding rose to 71.7%, up from 47.4% for GPT-6, which OpenAI says means finer detail in photos, such as small text on a label or symbols on a sign. On especially difficult prompts, the model followed instructions 78.1% of the time, up from 62.5%. On expert-level tasks at the lowest reasoning setting it scored 65%, up from 52%. And on OpenAI's internal BrowseComp web research test at the Extra High setting, it scored 64.5% against 54.4% for GPT-6.

OpenAI also reports better results in teen safety evaluations, covering emotional reliance, disordered eating, dangerous activities, and age-restricted goods. In challenging conversations, the model met the company's safety policies 91% to 94% of the time, compared with 77% to 80% for GPT-6. That is an improvement, and it still leaves 6% to 9% of those difficult conversations where the model falls short.

The model picker

If your account has the update, the "Latest" option in the model picker now uses GPT-6.2 across the Instant, Medium, High, and Extra High thinking levels. The Pro thinking level still uses GPT-6 Pro, as there is no GPT-6.2 Pro yet.

What it means for you

If you pay for ChatGPT, the chat you already use gets noticeably better at two things people actually do in it: asking about a photo, and giving a long, fiddly brief and expecting it to be followed. The price-drop follow-up is the more interesting shift. ChatGPT is starting to offer to watch something for you after the conversation ends, which is a small step toward it acting between chats rather than only inside them.

If you are on Free or the cheaper Go plan, nothing changes yet. Those accounts stay on GPT-6 Luna, which reached regular chat in October, and OpenAI has given no date for GPT-6.2 there. And if you use ChatGPT Work or Codex, this release does not touch you at all; the Astra and Sol models from November 17 stay as they are.

One date to note: if your model picker still shows GPT-5.6 Sol, you can keep using it in Chat until December 23, when OpenAI retires GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra from Chat, Work, and Codex.

Warning

Every performance number in this article comes from OpenAI's own testing, and none of it has been independently verified. Treat the jumps as a direction, not a measurement. OpenAI's own system card for the Chat build also rates the model at Critical for cybersecurity capability and High for biological capability under its Preparedness Framework, which is a statement about what the model can do, not a report of any incident.

What is still unclear: how fast the gradual rollout actually reaches accounts, and when, if ever, Free and Go users get GPT-6.2 in Chat. OpenAI has not said. Also unanswered is whether the proactive follow-ups, like price-drop alerts, will stay a convenience or become a way for shopping and travel partners to reach into the chat, since the same update arrives alongside OpenAI's push into agentic shopping.
