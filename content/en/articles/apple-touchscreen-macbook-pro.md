## What happened

Apple has finally put a touchscreen on a Mac. At its "Pro and tell" event in Los Angeles on October 28, 2026, the company unveiled the MacBook Pro with ==PROLED XDR Display==, the first Mac laptop with touch input, alongside a tandem OLED panel, a Dynamic Island, and M5 Pro and M5 Max chips.

The reversal is the story as much as the hardware. Apple spent years arguing that touch belongs on iPad, not Mac, and its one earlier experiment, the Touch Bar, was removed after a mixed reception. Now touch arrives as a full input method, positioned as an addition to the keyboard and trackpad rather than a replacement.

## The display is the real story

PROLED XDR is a tandem OLED panel, meaning two self-illuminating pixel layers instead of a backlit mini-LED array. Apple rates it at up to 2,000 nits of peak HDR and SDR brightness and a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, with ProMotion refresh from 10Hz to 120Hz and coverage of both P3 and Adobe RGB. In practice that means true blacks with no halo around bright objects, and a reference-grade screen for photo and video work on the go. An optional nano-texture finish cuts glare further.

Touch itself is built into the panel rather than layered on top, which Apple says reduces latency so swipes feel like touching the content directly. Everyday gestures cover the obvious: swipe to scroll a page, pinch to zoom a photo, drag slides between presentations, double-tap a window to fill the screen. For pro work, designers can draw on the full display instead of being limited to the trackpad area, and musicians can play software instruments onscreen. A redesigned hinge keeps the screen from wobbling when touched, and video stabilization smooths the camera feed during calls.

:::ringkasan
The headline is touch, but the display upgrade may matter more day to day: tandem OLED, 2,000 nits, and a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio in a laptop that is also thinner and lighter than before.
:::

The notch is gone, replaced by a ==Dynamic Island== that hosts Spotlight, Live Activities, and Siri AI. The 14-inch model weighs 3 pounds and the 16-inch 4 pounds, both lighter than their predecessors, with up to 24 hours of battery life. Ports stay generous: three Thunderbolt 5 ports, HDMI, an SDXC slot, MagSafe, and a headphone jack.

## What it means for you

Start with the lineup, because Apple now sells three distinct MacBook Pros and they are easy to confuse. The $1,999 entry-level 14-inch model announced October 27 runs an M6 chip with the older design, no OLED, no touch. The existing mini-LED M5 Pro and M5 Max models stay on sale as the cheaper pro option. The new touchscreen model sits at the top: $2,899 for the 14-inch and $3,399 for the 16-inch with M5 Pro, rising to $4,499 and $4,799 with M5 Max.

That top end is where the sticker shock lives. A fully loaded 14-inch reaches $14,249 and the 16-inch $14,549, driven by the 16TB storage option and 128GB of memory. The previous 16-inch topped out at $10,149, so the ceiling rose by $4,400. Some of that is the costlier OLED panel; some is the ongoing memory shortage and AI hardware demand pushing component prices up.

So who should buy it? If you draw, grade color, or play instruments onscreen, touch plus the OLED panel is a genuine workflow change, and the apps are ready: Canva, Zoom, Word, PowerPoint, Lightroom, DaVinci Resolve, and Blender are all touch-compatible at launch, with macOS translating touches into clicks for everything else. If you mostly write code and documents, the M6 entry model or a discounted mini-LED model buys you nearly the same performance for far less money, and touch adds little.

What is still unclear: Apple has not announced pricing or availability for Indonesia, so the dollar figures above are US prices. Independent reviews and real-world battery tests are also still to come, and any talk of touch coming to the MacBook Air remains rumor until Apple says otherwise. Preorders opened October 28, with availability beginning November 6.
