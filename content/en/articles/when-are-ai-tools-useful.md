## The pattern

After trying many AI tools, the pattern is consistent. AI tools shine when three conditions hold: the task is specific, you can check the result yourself, and the cost of being wrong is small.

Summarizing meetings, formatting JSON, drafting emails, brainstorming product names. Here AI saves real time. It wobbles when the task is vague and the stakes are high: medical diagnosis, legal advice, investment decisions. In that territory it remains useful as a thinking partner, but not as a judge.

## Concrete examples

We apply the same principle to the tools we build ourselves. On the [[works|Works]] page, three are live and free:

Draw is a virtual whiteboard for diagrams and wireframes. It does not try to be everything; it does one thing, giving you a canvas for visual thinking, and does it well.

Toolkit holds 16 small utilities for developers: JSON formatting, diff checking, JWT decoding, UUID generation, Base64 conversion, even image OCR. Each solves one specific task in seconds, right in your browser.

Pomodoro is a focus timer with audio cues and task tracking. Its job is narrow: helping you work in focused intervals. No grand claims.

All three follow the same rule: a clear task, immediately visible results, and you stay in control.

## How to use them in practice

Use AI for drafts, ideas, and exploration. Verify for facts, numbers, and decisions. And one question before using any tool: if the result is wrong, would I know? If the answer is no, treat the output as a suggestion, not the truth.

INTERPRETATION: the best AI tool is not the smartest one, but the most honest about its limits. Pick tools with narrow tasks and transparent results, and you will rarely be disappointed.
